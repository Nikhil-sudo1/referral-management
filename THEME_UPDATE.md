# Theme Update - Professional & Student-Friendly Design

## Overview
Updated the entire site theme to be:
- **Contrasting & Eye-Pleasing**: High contrast ratios for better readability
- **Student-Friendly**: Vibrant, approachable colors that appeal to students
- **Corporate-Friendly**: Professional blue primary color, trustworthy design

## Color Scheme Changes

### Primary Colors
- **Primary**: Professional corporate blue (`217 91% 60%`) - trustworthy and modern

### Background & Text
- **Background**: Clean, bright (`210 20% 98%`) - easy on the eyes

## Usage

The theme is automatically applied throughout the application. Key features:

- **Gradients**: Use `gradient-primary`, `gradient-success`, etc.
- **Cards**: Use `card-elevated` or `card-interactive` classes
- **Text**: Use `text-gradient` for gradient text effects
- **Hover**: Use `hover-lift` or `hover-glow` for interactive elements

## Color Accessibility

All colors meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards:
- Normal text: 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum
- Large text: 3:1 contrast ratio minimum
- Interactive elements: Clear focus states
