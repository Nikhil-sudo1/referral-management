## Application Context

This referral management platform enables TeamLease EdTech to:
1. Track student referrals from universities
2. Manage corporate partnership referrals
3. Monitor employability outcomes
4. Measure ROI for stakeholders
5. Coordinate between educators, students, and employers
6. Reward successful placements and admissions

## Integration Points

The platform aligns with TeamLease EdTech's core business by:
- Managing university student pipeline
- Tracking corporate training referrals
- Measuring conversion from education to employment
- Providing analytics on employability outcomes
- Facilitating the education-employment ecosystem
